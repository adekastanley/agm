project overview
 Hon. Amos Gwamna Magaji has a very compelling profile to work with: he represents the Zangon Kataf/Jaba constituency in Kaduna State, he’s a second-term member, and crucially, he is the Chairman of the House Committee on Health.  
 Because he is an incumbent running for re-election, the website cannot just be a static digital flyer; it needs to be an interactive "scorecard" that proves his value to his constituents. Building this with React and Vite is a smart move—it guarantees the blazing-fast, mobile-first performance required for a Nigerian audience, while the headless CMS will let his media team update the campaign trail in real-time.Here is a strategic blueprint for the website sections and how to structure the content models.1. The Hero Section (High-Impact & Action-Oriented)This needs to load instantly and establish authority.Dynamic Tagline: Something like "Continuing the Work. Delivering for Zangon Kataf & Jaba."High-Quality Portrait: A professional, approachable photo of Hon. Magaji.Primary Call-to-Actions (CTAs): Two clear buttons: "See My Impact" (scrolls to achievements) and "Join the Campaign" (links to volunteer form).Live Update Banner (CMS): A small, dismissible top-bar managed via the CMS for urgent updates (e.g., "Join us at the Zonkwa Townhall tomorrow at 10 AM!").2. Meet Hon. Amos Magaji (The Narrative)Voters connect with stories. His background is unique and should be highlighted.The Origin Story: Briefly touch on his birth in 1971 and his early dreams of medicine that led him to study Human Physiology.The Professional: Highlight his pre-politics career consulting in occupational health and safety across Abuja and Lagos.  The Leader: Frame his journey from private sector health consulting to becoming the Chairman of the House Committee on Health. It creates a narrative of lifelong dedication to public well-being.  3. The Impact Scorecard (CMS-Driven)This is the most critical section for an incumbent. Instead of just listing promises, you need to show receipts. This should be a dynamic React component (like a masonry grid or a filterable list) powered by the CMS.Legislative Interventions: Bills sponsored, motions moved, and his specific work pushing against the "japa" syndrome in Nigeria's health sector.  Constituency Projects: A visual gallery of built schools, boreholes, healthcare centers, or empowerment programs in Zangon Kataf/Jaba.Filter System: Allow users to filter his impact by category (e.g., Health, Education, Infrastructure, Youth Empowerment).4. The Health Agenda (Special Spotlight)Because he chairs the House Committee on Health, this deserves its own dedicated section. It elevates his profile from a local representative to a national leader.  Highlight national policies he is championing.Detail specific health missions or medical outreaches he has sponsored back home in Kaduna.5. Media & Campaign Hub (CMS-Driven)Since the campaign will generate daily content, this section needs to be highly dynamic.News & Press: Blog-style articles for official statements and press releases.The Campaign Trail (Gallery): A dynamic photo and video grid showing him on the ground interacting with constituents.Upcoming Events: A calendar or list of upcoming rallies, ward meetings, and town halls.6. Grassroots Mobilization (Interactive Forms)Convert website visitors into active campaign assets.Volunteer Registration: A form to capture data (Name, Ward, Polling Unit, Phone Number) for people wanting to canvas for him.Endorsements/Testimonials: Short quotes from community leaders and constituents about his impact.Feedback/Townhall Portal: A secure form where constituents can send messages, complain about local issues, or suggest ideas.Development Strategy Note:For the UI, you can leverage your preferred stack (like Tailwind CSS) alongside React to build reusable components (e.g., an ImpactCard, an EventBanner, a NewsThumbnail). Since you are using a headless CMS, you'll need to define schemas for Projects, News, Events, and Gallery so his team can update the site without touching the Vite codebase.
